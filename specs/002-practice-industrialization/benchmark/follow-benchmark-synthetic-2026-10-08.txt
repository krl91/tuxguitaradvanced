Tempo-following benchmark (spec 002 T101), run of 2026-10-08, 12 runs per cell, 16 events per run (chords: 8).
Synthetic audio (plucked-string synthesis, each note rings under the next, gain 0.3), analysed by the real chain, judged by the tempo-following session on a fake player
and clock; normal tolerance; no latency compensation. SC-009 (provisional): >= 95 % of correctly played notes matched to the right event; >= 95 % of runs stay
synchronised after a deliberate mistake. SC-004: repeated identical notes at 8 a second separated in >= 95 % of cases.
A match is right when the event is MATCHED and its offset is within 60 ms of the time the note was played (the 25 ms of jitter and the detection bias are inside the offset).
