Code revision: 17065738b (+ uncommitted changes)
Data: IDMT-SMT-Guitar V2 dataset 1, plain single notes (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, evaluation only, kept outside the repository); 236 passages of 4 guitars. Settings: RecognitionSettings.defaults(), PolyphonicAcceptance.Parameters.defaults().
A note is recognised when accepted in its turn between 50 ms before and 0.6 s after its onset (read from the annotation). False accept: the last note expected is a wrong one (two semitones from the real one) and is accepted.
Targets of the specification: SC-002 at least 95 % of the notes recognised at 8 notes per second and 90 % at 12, false accepts under 1 %; SC-004 identical repeats at 8 notes per second separated in at least 95 % of the passages.
