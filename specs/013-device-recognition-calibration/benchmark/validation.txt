== What could NOT be measured, and why
   SC-005 / SC-006 (the rate with the default and with the profile on the owner's recording of basic_test.tg, then the 22 built-in-microphone notes judged with the profile found):
   NOT RUN. The owner's recording (T004) does not exist: ~/tuxguitar-owner-takes-backup/calibration/ holds only the recording tool (record-basic-test.sh), no take.

   The search was not tuned on any validation data.

   3. Append the figures here (default vs profile: calibration recording, the 22 notes, the chord takes), with the scope: this built-in microphone and this guitar only.
