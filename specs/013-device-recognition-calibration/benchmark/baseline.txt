Bug found by the owner on the built application, 2026-10-19: cancelling the calibration gave an error and the application stopped answering. Cause (reproduced first by CalibrationWindowSwtTest on the real SWT toolkit, a StackOverflowError): when the window ended it called window.close(), which fires its own close listener, which called close() again, without end; the interface thread died while the capture and analysis threads kept the process alive. Fix in the code: CalibrationWindow disposes the window instead of closing it. The headless toolkit could not see it (its close does not fire the listener); the native test now covers the cancel button at the offer, tuning, advice and piece steps and the cross with the question Stop the calibration? (No keeps the window, Yes cancels). Whole suite after the fix: practice 1057 (6 skipped), engine 373, 0 failures.
