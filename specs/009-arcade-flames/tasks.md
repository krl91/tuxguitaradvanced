## Phase 3: User Story 1 - The flames show how well I play (P1) MVP

**Goal**: flames burn at the bottom of the picture, their height follows the **real** share of the maximum (embers at 50 % or less, full at 100 %, linear), they start as embers and wait for 4 judged events, they rise in about 0.5 s and fall in about 3 s (FR-001 to FR-004, FR-006, SC-001, SC-003).

**Independent Test**: a recorded run at low, medium and high success gives ordered heights (reference heights of SC-001); the same run with the setting off is identical in everything else (SC-004, SC-005).

- [ ] T006 [P] [US1] Write `TST/arcade/view/FlameSceneTest.java` first (red): for the three tiers and the three settings, the number of tongues and layers (`FULL` 28 tongues of 3 layers plus embers and the base glow, `BALANCED` 14 tongues of 2 layers and no glow, `PERFORMANCE` none, `DISCREET` half the tongues at 60 % of the height and 60 % of the opacity of `FULL`); at level 0 only embers; the maximum height of the tongues is ordered and proportional for levels 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1; **in the middle of the width the height and the opacity are at most 22 % of those at the corners, and the corner tongues at level 1 are at most 55 % of the picture height** (FR-001); `OFF` or a null state gives exactly the item list of the scene without the feature (SC-005); the same inputs give the same items and a different flame clock changes only the sway; with `motion` false the sway is the same whatever the clock; every flame item is **before** every other item of the list (contracts/arcade-flames.md invariants 1 to 4, 6).
- [ ] T007 [P] [US1] Write `TST/arcade/ui/FlameLevelTest.java` first (red): the share is the **real ratio** score / maximum (a share of exactly 0.5 gives 0 and of exactly 1.0 gives 1, 0.8 gives 0.6; `maximum` 0 gives share 0); fewer than 4 judged events: target 0, also for a whole run of fewer than 4; from 0 to 1 in 0.5 s and from 1 to 0 in 3 s, each within 20 %; at 60 pictures a second the step is at most 4 % of the range when rising and 1 % when falling; never a step larger than the rate times the time elapsed; `hold` keeps the level and `resume` goes on without a jump; a new `FlameLevel` starts at 0 (invariant 7).
