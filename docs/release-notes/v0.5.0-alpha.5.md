## 🎸 TuxGuitarAdvanced 0.5.0-alpha.5 — play your tablature like a game

**Open a tab. Plug in your guitar. Notes fly towards the neck: play them when they land.**

<p align="center"><img src="https://github.com/krl91/tuxguitaradvanced/raw/master/docs/images/manual-en/arcade-run.png" width="760" alt="Arcade mode: notes coming from the horizon in a straight line towards their fret on the neck"></p>

TuxGuitarAdvanced adds **practice modes** on top of [TuxGuitar](https://www.tuxguitar.app/), the free multitrack tablature editor you may already know: it listens to your real guitar, checks the **pitch** you play and the **timing**, and tells you right away. This new alpha makes **Arcade** — the game-like mode — show **every technique written in the score**.

### 🎮 Try it in 30 seconds

1. Open any tablature (a Guitar Pro file works).
2. Put the cursor on the track you want to play.
3. Click the **coloured button** in the transport bar (next to Play). The game opens at once, with your last audio input.
4. Play the notes as they reach the neck. **Restart** is one click away; **Pause** and **Stop** too.

No guitar at hand? Open the tutorial in the [manual](https://github.com/krl91/tuxguitaradvanced/blob/master/docs/MANUAL.md#b6-arcade-mode-alpha) and watch how the notes come, even without playing.

### ✨ New in this alpha

* **Bends you can see coming.** A dashed arrow on the note while it travels, then a **ghost rectangle** on the neck at the fret the bend must reach, with the amount: ¼, ½, *full*, 1½… and *pre* / ↓ for pre-bends and releases.
* **Hammer-ons, pull-offs, slides** show where they go (ghost on the next note of the string, dashed arc or rail with H, P, S), **vibrato** is a wave, **palm mute** and **tapping** are little pills, **tremolo picking** is three strokes, a **harmonic** says *Harmonic ◇*.
* A picture that is **calmer and more tilted by default**, and **yours to adjust**: size of the notes, scale and height of the horizon.
* **One-click start**, **Restart** inside the game, and a **default input** that is remembered (and picked again when you plug your interface back in).

### 🤝 This is an alpha, and I need you

It works, it is tested, and it is **not finished**. I would love to know:

* Does it **hear your guitar properly**? Electric, acoustic, bass, which microphone or interface?
* Is the **picture readable** when it gets busy (fast passages, chords, bends)? Too small, too much, too little?
* Are the **technique marks** useful, or noise? Which ones would you change?
* **Where did you get lost** — the first launch, the input, the settings, the manual?
* **What do you wish it did** that it does not?

👉 **[Open an issue](https://github.com/krl91/tuxguitaradvanced/issues/new)** — a screenshot, your guitar and your audio input, and what happened is more than enough. Even "it felt great" or "I did not get it" helps.

### ⚠️ Honest notes before you start

* **macOS on Apple Silicon only** for now (Linux and Windows builds exist but are not attached to this alpha). The app is signed ad hoc and **not notarised**: the first time, allow it in *System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway*. It asks for the **microphone** to listen to your guitar.
* The game **judges timing and pitch**. It does **not** tell you whether a hammer-on, a slide or a bend was *heard*: the drawings show what the score asks for, and the detectors are not validated yet. The [manual](https://github.com/krl91/tuxguitaradvanced/blob/master/docs/MANUAL.md#b6-arcade-mode-alpha) has a table of what is drawn and what is checked.
* Not validated yet by other players, guitars or machines — that is exactly what this alpha is for. Bass notes below G1 are shown but not judged, and you cannot hear your own guitar through the app (use your amp or headphones on your interface).
* The dashed lines of the technique marks have been checked by tests and rendered pictures, but not yet by many eyes on the real canvas.

### 🙏 Built on TuxGuitar

All the editing, playback and file formats come from **TuxGuitar** and its many contributors; this fork only adds the practice modes and a steadier tuner. If you only want a superb editor, [go and use TuxGuitar](https://www.tuxguitar.app/) too.
