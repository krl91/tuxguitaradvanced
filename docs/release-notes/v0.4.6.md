## TuxGuitarAdvanced 0.4.6

Bends are now followed and required in the practice mode, the practice says when it cannot follow, and two start-up errors are fixed.

### New

* **Bends in practice.** A note with a written bend is no longer passed automatically. It starts on its written pitch and is accepted when the bend **reaches its target**; the practice tells you to bend up to the target note. While you bend, no pitch on the way is shown as a wrong note, the pitch the bend reaches is not taken for the next note (which needs its own pick), and **Skip** passes a bend you do not want to play.
* **Release and pre-bend.** A written release must be made too (the practice tells you to release down to its end); a pre-bend is expected at its bent pitch. A bend that leaves its pitch at once, a signal that drops out for a moment and a guitar that reads a little sharp no longer block the note. Messages in English, French and Spanish.
* **The practice says when it cannot follow.** It tells you when notes are closer than it can follow (about 0.3 s between picked notes), shows the measured delay of the recognition in the preparation window, and warns when the input is too weak for reliable fast recognition.
* **Inputs.** Inputs are accepted, shown and selectable whatever their type (built-in, USB, Bluetooth): nothing is refused because of a name.

### How the practice recognises the written techniques

The practice **listens to the pitch** of what you play, with the pitch as its single source. It cannot tell the string or the fret from the sound, so those stay an instruction of the tablature. For the techniques written in the score it follows **how the pitch moves**:

| Written technique | What the practice recognises in 0.4.6 |
|---|---|
| **Bend** | The note starts on its fretted pitch. The practice follows the pitch rising along the written path (a bend value is a quarter of a tone: 4 is a whole tone) and **accepts the note when the pitch reaches the target** and holds it. Until then it says *"X is heard: now bend it up to Y"*. The target is met within 30 cents (or 15 % of the bend) below and half a semitone above. A bend that stops halfway, or is not played, keeps the practice on that note until you play it or press **Skip**. |
| **Pre-bend** | The practice expects the **bent** pitch from the first sound (the string is bent before it is struck), with the same tolerance as the target of a bend. The fretted pitch played plain is not the pre-bend. |
| **Release** | After the rise, the pitch must **come down to the written end** (half a semitone on each side) before the note is accepted: *"now release the bend down to Y"*. The fall is not accepted before the rise. |
| **Hammer-on, pull-off** | The destination note is accepted **from its pitch, without a new pick**, so a legato note is not asked to be picked again (measured on synthetic signals at about 0.1 s after the step; not yet on a real recording). |
| **Slide** | The note the slide arrives on is accepted from its pitch without a new pick. A slide played as steps can still show its middle note as a wrong note (see the limits). |
| **Vibrato** | Not required: the note is accepted from its pitch whether or not you play the vibrato. |
| **Palm mute, tremolo picking, tapping, harmonics** | Not judged yet (a written harmonic is passed automatically). |

For every one of them the practice also shows a **technique line** under the pitch result. In this version it says *cannot be checked yet*: the detectors that would say *heard* or *not heard* (measured on annotated recordings: vibrato 59 %, bend 82 %, slide 48 %, against a target of 85 %) are **not validated enough on real guitar recordings to show a verdict**, so none is shown. Do not read that line as a failure. What the practice **does** enforce for a bend is the **pitch path** above, because it comes from the pitch, which it measures reliably; it does not judge *how* the bend was made (finger vibrato in a bend, for instance, cannot be told from the pitch alone).

### Fixes

* **An error box (`ConcurrentModificationException`) could appear when a Guitar Pro file was opened at start-up.** The tool bar read the song while it was still being loaded; it now waits for the loading to finish.
* **A `NullPointerException` of the built-in synthesizer at every file opening** (a race of the upstream code: the soundbank of the replaced song answered after it was closed). Stale answers are now ignored and the instruments of the new song are loaded.

### Under the hood (not usable yet)

* The groundwork of the tempo-following mode: the player's clock and the capture's clock, the timeline of the score and the timing tolerances (strict, normal, lenient, provisional values). Nothing of it is shown in the application yet.
* A player-only mute of a track in the player (nothing changes unless it is asked).

### Honest limits

* **No technique detector is verified yet**: the technique line still says *cannot be checked yet* for bend, vibrato, hammer-on, pull-off and slide, and never says *heard*. What is new is that a bend is **followed and required**, not that it is judged for accuracy.
* The bends were validated on the owner's guitar on test scores (rising, descending, pre-bend with release) and on synthetic and rendered audio. **Vibrato, hammer-on, slide, a bend deliberately not played, and other guitars and microphones are not validated.**
* A pre-bend **without** a release has no arrow in the TuxGuitar score, so it looks like a plain note while the practice expects the bent pitch.
* A guitar tuned about 35 cents sharp or more passes plain notes only at the edge of the tolerance: tune with the tuner first.
* A slide played as steps (as some notation programs render it) can show its middle note as a wrong note.
* The Android build was not run for this release (no Android SDK on the development machine); the packages are macOS (Apple Silicon), Linux and Windows.
