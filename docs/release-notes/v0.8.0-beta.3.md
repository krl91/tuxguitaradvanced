## 🎸 TuxGuitarAdvanced 0.8.0-beta.3 — the demo fire burns on the score while you practise

TuxGuitarAdvanced adds **practice modes** on top of [TuxGuitar](https://www.tuxguitar.app/), the free multitrack tablature editor: it listens to your real guitar, checks the **pitch of the notes and the chords** you play against the tablature, and lets you practise note by note, in time (Follow, Arcade) or waiting for the right note.

### 🔖 About this release

A **pre-release** for feedback, with the first part of spec 019: the Amiga demo fire of Arcade now also burns **at the bottom of the score** in **Wait** and **Follow the tempo**. It is **off by default**. The rest of spec 019 (sparks on the score, an *Advanced* tab to tune the fire, *Reduce motion* read on Windows and Linux) comes in a later release.

Every feature is in all three packages (macOS, Linux and Windows), with the same behaviour.

> 🙏 **Windows and Linux users: your feedback is needed.** The author has no Windows or Linux computer at hand, so these packages are built by the CI but **have never been run**. If you try one, please say what works and what does not (launch, microphone, tuner, practice modes, Arcade) in an [issue](https://github.com/krl91/tuxguitaradvanced/issues), with your system and its version.

### ✨ What is new since 0.8.0-beta.2

* **A fire on the score** in Wait and Follow: it rises from the bottom corners of the score, **grows with your streak of right notes** (full height after 16 in a row) and **falls back at a mistake** (a wrong note, a skipped note, a missed note).
* It never covers the **framed note**, the **next note** or the **practice palette**: the fire fades out around them.
* It stays within the bottom third of the score, holds still while the run is paused, and goes away when the run ends.
* **Choose it** in the practice window, *Mode* tab, *Score fire*: *Off* (default), *Discreet* or *Full*.
* The verdicts, the timing and the summary are exactly the same with the fire on or off.
* The score is not redrawn for each picture of the fire, so the fire costs little: about 3 ms per picture at *Full* on the author's Mac (median; up to about 4.4 ms for the slowest 5 %).

### ✅ What was checked, and by whom

* **Tests:** the full macOS build is green: 1,371 practice tests, 378 audio-engine tests and 42 tests on the real SWT toolkit, 0 failures. Real-toolkit tests check that the fire is painted over the score at the bottom only, that nothing is added when it is off, and that the score is not repainted for a picture of the fire alone.
* **Measured by the author's build** on macOS: the cost of the fire on the real score (above).
* **Not checked by anyone yet:** the score fire in a real practice run (it is what the author tries now); the palette that moves aside (sheet F10); the **Windows and Linux packages were never run** (built only).

### 🚧 What is **not** fixed

* Notes and chords whose **attack is not heard** (a soft note, a low input level) are still refused.
* A chord with **one string missing** can still be accepted.
* *Reduce motion* is read from the system on macOS only; on Windows and Linux the fire always moves.
* The macOS package is **not notarised**: the first launch needs *Open Anyway* (System Settings → Privacy & Security). The Windows package needs Java 17 or later.
