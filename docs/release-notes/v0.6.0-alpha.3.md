## 🎸 TuxGuitarAdvanced 0.6.0-alpha.3 — Arcade in five levels, and a more forgiving Arcade

TuxGuitarAdvanced adds **practice modes** on top of [TuxGuitar](https://www.tuxguitar.app/), the free multitrack tablature editor: it listens to your real guitar, checks the **pitch of the notes and the chords** you play against the tablature, and lets you practise note by note, in time (Follow, Arcade) or waiting for the right note.

### ✨ What is new in this alpha

* **Five levels in Arcade**, so that a hard piece becomes playable. **Every start of Arcade asks the level** on the first line of the small start window (level 1 the first time, then the last one you chose):
  * **level 1**: about 30 % of the notes and **no chord**; **level 2**: 30 % of the notes and 30 % of the chords; **level 3**: 50 % and 50 %; **level 4**: 75 % and 75 %; **level 5**: the whole piece, as before.
  * the **first sound of each kind is always kept** and the others are spread evenly after it, the **same ones at every run**; a chord is kept or removed whole; a run is never empty.
  * the sounds a level leaves out are **not shown, not expected and not judged**, and the score is counted on the sounds kept. **The band plays them for you** (with the instrument of your track), so the music stays complete. The result window says which level was played.
* **A more forgiving Arcade, still adjustable.** In the *Mode* tab, with Arcade chosen, a **Permissiveness** line has three positions: *Like Practice* (exactly the recognition and the timing of the practice modes), *Fun* (the default) and *Very permissive*. It applies to Arcade **only** (Follow, Wait and the tuner are unchanged), only relaxes and never tightens what you or a calibration profile already set, widens the timing window (by a quarter or a half, never beyond 200 ms) and relaxes some recognition thresholds.
* Texts in English, French and Spanish; the guide `docs/PRACTICE.md` describes the levels and the permissiveness.

### ✅ What was checked, and by whom

* **Tests:** the full build is green (more than 1,140 practice tests and 373 audio-engine tests), with native window tests on macOS. **Follow, Wait, the tuner and the calibration are unchanged**, and the recognition code is identical to the previous release.
* **macOS (Apple Silicon):** the author tried **choosing the level** in the start window on the built application and it works. Nothing else was tried by hand yet: not a full run at each level, not the permissiveness, not the sound of the removed sounds.
* **Measured, and honestly:** on the reference recording and on the built-in-microphone notes, the more forgiving recognition recognises **exactly the same notes as before** (35 of 38 judged, 0 wrong notes accepted of 98 tests, the same 21 of 21 notes on the microphone) and it does **not** make the verdict quicker: the delay (about 105 ms) is fixed by the attack detector and is not changed. The forgiveness you feel in Arcade therefore comes mostly from the **wider timing window** and from the **levels**. Whether the relaxed thresholds help on a weaker input is **not measured yet**.
* **The sounds that a level removes are played by the band at their written time.** Their scheduling was measured (a few milliseconds), but their **offset against the sounds of the band, and how a bend or a slide sounds in them, have not been listened to by anyone yet** (they were sent through the player's output, as the editor does to audition a beat): tell us if they do not sound in time.
* **Not checked by anyone yet:** the Windows and Linux packages were never run (their contents were listed: they contain TarsosDSP and the three practice modules).

### 🚧 What is **not** fixed

* A chord with **one string missing** can still be accepted: nothing here changes the recognition algorithms.
* The practice modes, the calibration and the Arcade levels exist only in the macOS, Linux and Windows **SWT** packages (not in the JavaFX, Qt or FreeBSD builds).
* The macOS package is signed with a self-made certificate and not notarised (the first launch needs *Open Anyway*); the Windows package needs Java 17 or later.

**Please try it and tell us** whether Arcade is fun at the default position and whether the sounds left out by a level sound in time: open an [issue](https://github.com/krl91/tuxguitaradvanced/issues).
