## 🎸 TuxGuitarAdvanced 0.8.0-beta.1 — the practice palette moves out of the way, and Arcade's flames become a demo-scene fire

TuxGuitarAdvanced adds **practice modes** on top of [TuxGuitar](https://www.tuxguitar.app/), the free multitrack tablature editor: it listens to your real guitar, checks the **pitch of the notes and the chords** you play against the tablature, and lets you practise note by note, in time (Follow, Arcade) or waiting for the right note.

### 🔖 About this release

A **pre-release** for feedback. Both changes are built and tested, but **neither has been tried by the author yet** (test sheets F10 and F06). The look of the fire (pixel size, colours, transparency) may still change after that try.

Every feature is in all three packages (macOS, Linux and Windows), with the same behaviour.

> 🙏 **Windows and Linux users: your feedback is needed.** The author has no Windows or Linux computer at hand, so these packages are built by the CI but **have never been run**. If you try one, please say what works and what does not (launch, microphone, tuner, practice modes, Arcade) in an [issue](https://github.com/krl91/tuxguitaradvanced/issues), with your system and its version.

### ✨ What is new since 0.7.0-beta.1

* **The practice palette moves aside** in *Wait for the right note* and *Follow the tempo*. When the note to play, or the next one, comes under the small palette, the palette jumps to a free corner of the score, ahead of time, **without taking the keyboard focus** (the practice shortcuts keep working without a click).
  * It does not go back and forth: it never returns to the place it just left while the notes are still near it, and moves at most once per note.
  * If no corner is free, it takes the one that hides the least.
  * If you drag it somewhere, it stays there until a note comes under it. If you put it beside the main window, it never moves.
  * Arcade is not affected.
* **Arcade's stage flames are now a fire in big pixels**, like the classic fire of the Amiga demos. Square cells go from dark red to white at the base, flickering and rising.
  * The fire is **see-through** (at most 60 % opaque), so the neck and the notes always show through it.
  * Each **right note throws a burst of sparks**, more of them with a longer streak; they fade within a second (highest quality only).
  * The *Stage flames* setting is unchanged: *Off*, *Discreet* (the default), *Full*. The fire still grows with your share of the maximum score, never changes the judging, fades near every text, and stands still with *Reduce motion*.
* Texts in English, French and Spanish; `docs/PRACTICE.md` describes both changes.

### ✅ What was checked, and by whom

* **Tests:** the full macOS build is green: about 1,320 practice tests, 378 audio-engine tests and 37 tests on the real SWT toolkit, 0 failures.
  * Every existing flame test still passes unchanged: heights, corners and middle, discreet, off, the contrast of the texts, and that the judging is unchanged.
  * The palette's move was checked on real SWT: the focused control and the active window stay the same.
* **Not checked by anyone yet:** both changes by hand (the author's try is to come); the **Windows and Linux packages were never run** (built only).

### 🚧 What is **not** fixed

* Notes and chords whose **attack is not heard** (a soft note, a low input level) are still refused.
* A chord with **one string missing** can still be accepted.
* The macOS package is **not notarised**: the first launch needs *Open Anyway* (System Settings → Privacy & Security). The Windows package needs Java 17 or later.
