## TuxGuitarAdvanced 0.4.5

Spanish translation, a complete French practice window, and a fuller documentation.

### New

* **Spanish (español)**: the practice mode, the tuner and 114 strings of the application that were still in English in Spanish (measure errors, effects, dynamics, toolbar, tuning presets...) are translated. Choose *Español* in *Tools > Settings > Languages* and restart.
* **Spanish documentation**: [user guide](https://github.com/krl91/tuxguitaradvanced/blob/master/docs/PRACTICE-ES.md) and an in-application help page, *Reconocimiento de audio*.

### Fixes

* **The practice window was partly in English in French**: 107 strings (track choice, input messages, feedback, passages, pauses...) were missing from the French file and are added.
* The documentation no longer says that written techniques are not judged: for each one a line under the pitch result says what was heard, or "cannot be checked yet" (no technique detector is verified yet).

### Also in this release

* Documentation: full tuner section and screenshots of the tuner, the practice windows and the audio recognition settings, in the English, French and Spanish guides.
* Continuous integration: the upstream workflows keep their artifacts for one day only.

Not translated: the General MIDI instrument and percussion names and the language names, which the upstream project leaves in English. See [0.4.4](https://github.com/krl91/tuxguitaradvanced/releases/tag/v0.4.4) for the macOS, Linux and Windows packages and the technique-detection limits, and [0.4.2](https://github.com/krl91/tuxguitaradvanced/releases/tag/v0.4.2) for the measured recognition quality and the installation notes.

**Full changelog**: https://github.com/krl91/tuxguitaradvanced/compare/v0.4.4...v0.4.5
