## 🎸 TuxGuitarAdvanced 0.5.0-beta.1 — a new face for the game

<p align="center"><img src="https://github.com/krl91/tuxguitaradvanced/raw/master/specs/008-brand-identity/assets/tuxguitaradvanced-logo-primary.png" width="420" alt="TuxGuitarAdvanced: the Tux penguin with a red guitar in front of a rainbow note highway"></p>

**This is the first beta, and it carries the official logo of TuxGuitarAdvanced**: the Tux penguin with his guitar, the six coloured strings and the notes flying towards you.

TuxGuitarAdvanced adds **practice modes** on top of [TuxGuitar](https://www.tuxguitar.app/), the free multitrack tablature editor you may already know: it listens to your real guitar, checks the **pitch** you play and the **timing**, and tells you right away.

### ✨ What is new in this beta

* **The new logo, everywhere the application shows its identity:** the application icon (Dock, Finder, window title bar), the splash screen, the Windows installer images and icons, the Linux package icon, and the READMEs. The small icons use the **icon-only version of the logo** (the penguin, the guitar and the note highway, without the lettering, which cannot be read at 16 to 64 pixels); the splash and the large icons show the complete logo.
* **No change to how it works.** The beta is the alpha.6 game and practice modes (chords and notes landing together at the foot of the neck, the count-in during which the notes come from the far end, the result window when you stop, the real dashes of the bend and technique marks) with a new face: no program code of the application was changed for the logo.
* A test of the logo files and a check of every package (macOS, Windows, Linux) now run in the release workflow, so a package that would miss the new icons is not published.

### ✅ What was checked, and by whom

* **macOS (Apple Silicon):** the owner looked at the built application: the icon in the Dock and in the application switcher, in the title bar, and the splash screen.
* **Windows and Linux:** the packages are built by the workflow and **the icons are verified by script** (they are inside the packages and identical to the files of the repository). **Nobody has looked at how they appear** on a Windows or a Linux machine: tell me if something looks wrong (icon too small, blurred, on an opaque square).
* The logo is an **AI-assisted redesign selected by the product owner**. The upstream TuxGuitar notices and licence are unchanged, and no ownership of third-party marks is claimed.

### 🎮 Try it in 30 seconds

1. Open any tablature (a Guitar Pro file works).
2. Put the cursor where you want to start, on the track you want to play.
3. Click the **coloured button** in the transport bar (next to Play). The game opens at once with your last audio input.
4. Listen to the clicks, play the notes as they land.

### ⚠️ Honest notes before you start

* **macOS on Apple Silicon only** is looked at by a human for now. The app is signed ad hoc and **not notarised**: the first time, allow it in *System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway*. It asks for the **microphone** to listen to your guitar. On macOS the Finder may keep showing the old icon of a copy of the application until you move or copy it again.
* The game **judges timing and pitch**. It does **not** tell you whether a hammer-on, a slide or a bend was *heard*: the drawings show what the score asks for, and the detectors are not validated yet. The modes are still called alpha in the documentation for that reason.
* Bass notes below G1 are shown but not judged, and you cannot hear your own guitar through the app (use your amp or headphones on your interface).

### 🙏 Built on TuxGuitar

All the editing, playback and file formats come from **TuxGuitar** and its many contributors; this fork only adds the practice modes and a steadier tuner. If you only want a superb editor, [go and use TuxGuitar](https://www.tuxguitar.app/) too.
