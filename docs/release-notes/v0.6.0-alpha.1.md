## 🎸 TuxGuitarAdvanced 0.6.0-alpha.1 — calibrate the recognition for your own microphone or interface

TuxGuitarAdvanced adds **practice modes** on top of [TuxGuitar](https://www.tuxguitar.app/), the free multitrack tablature editor: it listens to your real guitar, checks the **pitch of the notes and the chords** you play against the tablature, and lets you practise note by note, in time (Follow, Arcade) or waiting for the right note.

### ✨ What is new in this alpha

* **Calibrate the recognition for one input.** In the **Input** tab of the practice window, a coloured **dot** next to the input chooser tells whether the selected input is calibrated: **grey** (no input), **orange** (not calibrated, the default settings are used), **green** (calibrated, at least 60 % of the sounds recognised), **red** (calibrated, but under 60 %). A sentence warns when the noise reduction or the gain is not what it was when you calibrated, and a profile made at another sample rate is not applied.
* **One window, step by step.** *Calibrate the recognition* opens a single window: the tuning question, advice, then a short piece to play once, with no tempo. A **picture of the tablature of the next sound to play** (six strings, the fret number on the string of each note, a chord in one column) changes each time a sound is heard. The window listens by itself and counts the sounds without saying which one it heard, shows the measures of the recording (sounds heard, highest level, background noise, clipping), and asks whether it is good enough.
* **The best settings for this input are searched on your recording** (at most about two minutes, the window blocks the application meanwhile) and the result shows the recognition rate with the default settings and with the settings found, for notes, chords and in total. The profile is kept per input and applies everywhere the recognition is used (tuner window, Follow, Arcade, Wait).
* **You always decide.** If the recording cannot be used, or the settings found are not better than the default, or better but under 60 %, you choose: record again, use the default configuration, or stop. Closing the window asks "Stop the calibration?" and then saves nothing. **Forget the calibration** deletes the profile of the selected input only.
* **No sound is kept** unless you tick, at the quality question, **Keep a copy of this recording for diagnosis**: a WAV file is then written in the folder `tuxguitar-calibration-takes` of your home folder (it is never read back and never sent anywhere).
* Texts in English, French and Spanish; a new section in the help page of the recognition settings.

### ✅ What was checked, and by whom

* **Tests:** the full build is green (more than 1,060 practice tests and 373 audio-engine tests), with native window tests on macOS. **With no profile, every recognition figure is equal to what it was before.** No recognition algorithm was changed: the calibration only chooses the settings.
* **macOS (Apple Silicon):** I tried the calibration on the built application, with the built-in microphone. A first trial showed a bug (cancelling the calibration froze the application): it is fixed and now covered by a native test.
* **Measured, and honestly:** on two takes of the calibration piece with the built-in microphone of this MacBook, the **default settings already recognise 13 sounds of 13**, so the search finds nothing better and keeps the default. **No gain was measured, because there was none to gain on this microphone and this guitar.** Whether the calibration helps on a microphone or an interface where the default settings recognise less is **not measured yet**: that is what this alpha is for.
* **Not checked by anyone yet:** the whole trial (calibrate, restart, another input, a refusal, the three-choice message, forgetting) by the owner; the **Windows and Linux packages were never run**; the picture of a chord was checked by its layout only.

### 🚧 What is **not** fixed

* A chord with **one string missing** can still be accepted: the calibration does not change the recognition algorithms.
* The practice modes and the calibration exist only in the macOS, Linux and Windows **SWT** packages of this release (not in the JavaFX, Qt or FreeBSD builds).
* The macOS package is signed with a self-made certificate and not notarised (the first launch needs *Open Anyway*); the Windows package needs Java 17 or later.

**Please try it and tell us** what the dot says for your input and whether the rate shown makes sense: open an [issue](https://github.com/krl91/tuxguitaradvanced/issues). The calibration can write no audio unless you tick the box above.
