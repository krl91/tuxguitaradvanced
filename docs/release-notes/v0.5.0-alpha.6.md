## 🎸 TuxGuitarAdvanced 0.5.0-alpha.6 — everything lands together, and the game starts the way a band does

**Open a tab. Plug in your guitar. Hear the clicks while the notes rise from the far end, then play them as they land.**

<p align="center"><img src="https://github.com/krl91/tuxguitaradvanced/raw/master/docs/images/release-alpha5/arcade-bend-ghost.png" width="760" alt="Arcade mode: a whole-tone bend on the neck with its ghost rectangle, a pre-bend and a release coming (screenshot of the previous alpha)"></p>

TuxGuitarAdvanced adds **practice modes** on top of [TuxGuitar](https://www.tuxguitar.app/), the free multitrack tablature editor you may already know: it listens to your real guitar, checks the **pitch** you play and the **timing**, and tells you right away. This alpha is a round of **playing-it-for-real fixes** of the **Arcade** mode, all of them reported while playing a demo song.

### ✨ What changed

* **Chords land like single notes.** A chord panel, an open-string bar and a timing bar now come down the same path as the footprint of a single note and **reach the foot of the neck at the same instant**; the rectangles of a chord land on their targets. Before, a chord arrived a little early, above the neck, and looked out of step with the tempo.
* **The game starts with the metronome.** During the count-in the first notes and the first chord **come from the far end** while you hear the clicks. Before, the first chord could already be on the neck when the game started, especially on a second run. After a **Pause**, **Resume** counts in again from where you were, and the picture holds still while paused.
* **The picture is never cut.** The streak, the tempo and *Next* at the top were partly hidden under the title bar; the game window is now **maximized** instead of full screen and laid out again at every resize. A test checks the texts for seven window shapes.
* **The result is shown when you stop too.** At the end of the song, even if nothing was played, **and when you press Stop or close the game**, the Result window appears. *From the start* replays from the first measure of the track and *From measure* honours the measure you choose (it used to start from where the last run began).
* **The approach band goes the way of the notes.** The light band behind the lanes stopped at the neck while the lanes went on; it now follows the same lines to the foot of the neck, so it points at the next note or chord.
* **Smaller things.** The note button of the transport bar is no longer left framed while the game hides the practice window, that window no longer pops up empty over the game, and the arrows, arcs and rails of the bend and technique marks are now drawn as **real dashes** (made of short segments of the path, because macOS ignores the dash style of its native painter).

### 🎮 Try it in 30 seconds

1. Open any tablature (a Guitar Pro file works).
2. Put the cursor where you want to start, on the track you want to play.
3. Click the **coloured button** in the transport bar (next to Play). The game opens at once with your last audio input.
4. Listen to the clicks, play the notes as they land. **Restart**, **Pause / Resume** and **Stop** are at the bottom right.

It works, it is tested, and it is **not finished**. I would love to know:

* Do the notes and chords **arrive in step with the tempo** now? Do you feel the foot of the neck as the place to play?
* Does the **count-in** feel natural, at the start and after a pause?
* Does the game ever **freeze or misbehave after several launches in a row**? I could not reproduce one report of it: if you see it, tell me **what you clicked, in which order**.
* Is the **picture readable** when it gets busy (fast passages, chords, bends)? Does your **guitar get heard properly** (electric, acoustic, bass, which interface)?

👉 **[Open an issue](https://github.com/krl91/tuxguitaradvanced/issues/new)** — a screenshot, your guitar and your audio input, and what happened is more than enough.

### ⚠️ Honest notes before you start

* **macOS on Apple Silicon only** for now (Linux and Windows builds exist but are not attached to this alpha). The app is signed ad hoc and **not notarised**: the first time, allow it in *System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway*. It asks for the **microphone** to listen to your guitar.
* The game **judges timing and pitch**. It does **not** tell you whether a hammer-on, a slide or a bend was *heard*: the drawings show what the score asks for, and the detectors are not validated yet.
* **Not yet seen on screen by the author:** the dashes of the technique marks on the native canvas, and the resume after a pause in every situation. The picture of the count-in after a resume goes back by one measure before coming again, on purpose, so that the notes arrive at their real moment.
* The first frame of a run is built when the window first paints, so a few tens of milliseconds of difference are possible at the end of the count-in.
* Bass notes below G1 are shown but not judged, and you cannot hear your own guitar through the app (use your amp or headphones on your interface).

### 🙏 Built on TuxGuitar

All the editing, playback and file formats come from **TuxGuitar** and its many contributors; this fork only adds the practice modes and a steadier tuner. If you only want a superb editor, [go and use TuxGuitar](https://www.tuxguitar.app/) too.
