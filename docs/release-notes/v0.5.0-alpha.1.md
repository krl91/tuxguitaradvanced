## TuxGuitarAdvanced 0.5.0-alpha.1

**First alpha of the Arcade mode and of the tempo-following mode.** The practice can now play the score at its tempo with the other tracks as a backing and judge your notes on time, and **Arcade** shows that run as a game: the notes come towards you on a neck from the horizon, five seconds ahead. This is an alpha: it works on the owner's Mac, it is **not** validated on other guitars, microphones or machines (see the limits below).

**Package:** only **macOS (Apple Silicon)** is attached. It was built on the owner's Mac from the code of this tag (the release workflow could not start: the project's GitHub Actions budget refuses jobs), with a Temurin 21 JRE embedded, signed ad hoc and **not notarised**: the first launch needs *Open Anyway* in *System Settings > Privacy & Security*. **There is no Linux and no Windows package for this alpha.**

### New

* **Follow the tempo.** In the *Mode* tab: the score plays at its tempo after a count-in, the track you practise is silent and the other tracks sound. Each note or chord is judged on time (on time, early, late, wrong, missed). Tempo from 20 % to 150 %, timing tolerance strict / normal / lenient, rhythm calibration of the audio route (clicks, or the delay typed by hand), and a summary at the end.
* **Arcade (alpha).** The same run on an isometric neck:
  * every note is seen coming **for five seconds**, from the horizon, whatever the number of notes; a note of fret *n* always follows the same lane to the middle of its fret; the string only gives the colour;
  * chords are timing bars with the named diagram of the next one; verdict badges after each judgement; streak, tempo and the next event at the top;
  * pause, resume and stop; a **result** window (score, share of the maximum, best streak, each judgement) and **play again** from the start or from a measure;
  * the **size of the incoming discs** is a setting (40 % to 150 %);
  * any pitched track, bass included (the lowest notes of a standard bass, below G1, are shown but not judged).
* **The preparation window is in four tabs** (Track, Input, Mode, Passages), sized for its widest and tallest tab, and recomposed when it comes back from an Arcade run.
* **A Refresh button** next to the input list lists the audio inputs again, for an interface plugged in while the application runs.
* **Documentation** with the images of the Arcade view in English, French and Spanish: [PRACTICE.md](https://github.com/krl91/tuxguitaradvanced/blob/master/docs/PRACTICE.md).

### Honest limits

* **Alpha.** The view and the run are checked by automatic tests (several hundred, including native SWT tests on the painted pixels and a replay of real scores) and by the owner on a Mac. Other players, guitars, microphones and machines have not tried it.
* **Timing under load:** in a 10-minute stress run the audio chain still misses about **one analysis deadline per minute** (a scheduling gap of the operating system, not of the application's logic). It was not shown to change a judgement, but the target is not met.
* **Hearing your own guitar through the application** is not offered: play through your amplifier or through headphones plugged into your audio interface.
* Notes closer than about 0.3 s may not be followed; the techniques (hammer-on, slide, bend, vibrato) are reported apart and do not change the Arcade score.
* The images in the documentation are renderings of the Arcade view by the application's renderer on test scores, not window captures.
* The Android build was not run for this release.
