## 🎸 TuxGuitarAdvanced 0.8.0-beta.2 — a denser, finer Amiga fire with many more sparks

TuxGuitarAdvanced adds **practice modes** on top of [TuxGuitar](https://www.tuxguitar.app/), the free multitrack tablature editor: it listens to your real guitar, checks the **pitch of the notes and the chords** you play against the tablature, and lets you practise note by note, in time (Follow, Arcade) or waiting for the right note.

### 🔖 About this release

A **pre-release** for feedback. The author tried the demo-scene fire of 0.8.0-beta.1 and found it too faint, too coarse and short of sparks; this release tunes it, and the author tried the tuned fire and kept it (test sheet F06). The palette that moves aside (0.8.0-beta.1) is unchanged and still waits for the author's try (sheet F10).

Every feature is in all three packages (macOS, Linux and Windows), with the same behaviour.

> 🙏 **Windows and Linux users: your feedback is needed.** The author has no Windows or Linux computer at hand, so these packages are built by the CI but **have never been run**. If you try one, please say what works and what does not (launch, microphone, tuner, practice modes, Arcade) in an [issue](https://github.com/krl91/tuxguitaradvanced/issues), with your system and its version.

### ✨ What is new since 0.8.0-beta.1

* **Arcade's fire is denser**: at most **85 %** opaque where it is hottest (60 % before). The top of the fire stays more see-through, and the fire still fades near every text.
* **Five times as many sparks**: 20 to 50 per right note, more with a longer streak, up to 300 in the air (highest quality only).
* **Finer big pixels**: the grid of the classic Amiga demo, a cell of 1/192 of the picture's width (about 7 pixels on a 1280-pixel-wide window, 11 before), with hard edges.
* The fire is now drawn much more cheaply, so the highest quality (the one with sparks) holds with the finer grid.

### ✅ What was checked, and by whom

* **Tests:** the full macOS build is green: 1,324 practice tests, 378 audio-engine tests and 37 tests on the real SWT toolkit, 0 failures. The real-toolkit test checks that the highest quality holds with the new fire.
* **Tried by the author** on macOS: the tuned fire in Arcade (*Full*, *Discreet*, *Off*): it works well.
* **Not checked by anyone yet:** the palette that moves aside (sheet F10); the **Windows and Linux packages were never run** (built only).

### 🚧 What is **not** fixed

* Notes and chords whose **attack is not heard** (a soft note, a low input level) are still refused.
* A chord with **one string missing** can still be accepted.
* *Reduce motion* is read from the system on macOS only; on Windows and Linux the fire always moves.
* The macOS package is **not notarised**: the first launch needs *Open Anyway* (System Settings → Privacy & Security). The Windows package needs Java 17 or later.
